## 🚀 Project Mission

**Freelancers in emerging markets lose 10–30% of their income to fees and slow transfers.**  
NomadXchange is a humanitarian-focused fintech wallet designed to fix that.

### ✨ What it does:
- 🔐 **Escrow contracts** that protect freelancers from non-payment
- 💸 **Global payouts** in USDC, powered by Stellar anchors
- ⏳ **Smart timers** for refunds and dispute protection
- 🌐 **Mobile-ready wallet** with Freighter & Stellar integration
- 💼 **Agency tools** for managing teams and milestones
